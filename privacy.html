Privacy policy: shorts-pipeline

Last updated: 20 November 2026

shorts-pipeline is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channels Night Shift Stories and Turno de Noche.

What data the app uses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos, read basic information about the owner's own channels, and update their description, keywords and banner.

What the app does not do

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
